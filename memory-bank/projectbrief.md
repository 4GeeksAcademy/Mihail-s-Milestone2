# Company context: TrackFlow

## What the company does

TrackFlow provides end-to-end logistics for e-commerce brands: inventory storage, order picking and packing, carrier handoff, shipment tracking, and returns. Its customers are brands (B2B); recipients are end consumers (B2C). The company's operating teams work across warehouse operations, carrier management, reverse logistics, customer experience, commercial/client relations, and technology.

## Current operating challenges

- Warehouse systems do not share a unified inventory view.
- Carrier assignment and tracking are handled manually across multiple carrier portals.
- Returns require individual human review.
- Customer support lacks a unified ticketing system and searchable knowledge base.
- Leadership reports are assembled manually and arrive late.
- Integrations and legacy systems are poorly documented, and cross-team incident visibility is limited.

## Direction

TrackFlow Tech is expected to build integrations, operational systems, shared data visibility, monitoring, and practical automation. Useful product surfaces include public shipment tracking, warehouse and carrier dashboards, returns workflows, and customer-experience tools. Treat these as company needs, not as authorization to invent an API or operational policy.
